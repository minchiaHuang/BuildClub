# Competitors

_Updated 4 Oct 2026 from the Miro board ("Market research: GEO / AEO startups, Oct 2026" and "Competitor tour: Gumshoe.ai"). The two full research reports the board cites (`Desktop/Research/reports`) are not on this machine. Prices marked (3P) come from third-party reviews._

## Bottom Line
Tracking whether AI mentions a brand is now table stakes. The open space is acting on it, proving it pays, or serving markets the big tools ignore.

## Market Map: See, Fix, Prove
| Layer | What | Crowding | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. See | Monitor AI answers: mentions, frequency, cited sources | Crowded; big tools sell it for $50–199/mo | Peec AI, Otterly.AI, Profound, Semrush, Ahrefs Brand Radar, HubSpot AEO |
| 2. Fix | Write content and change the site; agents publish after human approval | Hottest area in 2026; ~12 tools can auto-publish | AirOps, Search Atlas OTTO, Sitefire, Promptwatch, Scrunch AXP, Alli AI |
| 3. Prove | Link AI visibility to revenue | Almost nobody does this well | Petra Labs, Siftly (control tests), Wildcard (orders) |

## Gumshoe.ai Tour (closest competitor)
Free sample audit reviewed (brand: Vans). Features:
1. Visibility overview — score per buyer type, mention frequency, top brands
2. Competitor leaderboard — every brand ranked by AI mentions
3. AI conversations — the buyer's question, brands recommended in order, sources used
4. Citations — pages that mention rivals but not you; sites AI quotes, by type
5. Trends — visibility over time vs rivals; filter by AI tool, buyer type, topic
6. Buyer types (personas) — score split by buyer type
7. One buyer type in detail — who they are, sites AI uses for them, win/tie/lose per topic
8. Pulse audit — daily visibility and competitor ranking
9. Content audit — to-do list of pages to write or fix
10. Sentiment audit — positive/negative vs rivals

## Where Recite Differs
- **Honest measurement:** error ranges, non-answers excluded, no claim that personas differ unless the data shows it. Grounded in real flaws found in open-source tools (elmo counted failed answers as misses; ansvisor and Canonry misjudged names).
- **Evidence-linked fixes:** every recommendation points to a specific answer or cited site, addressing "tools show the score, not the fix".
- **Agency price point and AU focus:** built for Sydney agencies with 1–50 staff, priced per client.
- **Not a differentiator:** personas alone — Gumshoe and BrandViz already do it, and Recite's tests show intent matters more than persona.

## Opportunities (hypotheses, not validated)
1. **Trustworthy measurement** — the same prompt returns the same brand list less than 1% of the time (SparkToro). Closest: Evertune. Risk: will buyers pay for confidence intervals?
2. **Ecommerce and AI shopping** — few tools track ChatGPT Shopping, Amazon Rufus. Closest: Wildcard, Bluefish. Risk: platforms may build it.
3. **Revenue attribution** — nobody reliably links AI mentions to sales. Closest: Petra Labs ($15k/mo service), Siftly. Risk: AI referral traffic under 1%.
4. **Local languages and engines** — Western tools skip Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Doubao, DeepSeek, Naver. Closest: awoo, Emergine. Risk: smaller budgets.
5. **Agent experience for dev tools** — coding agents now choose tools. Closest: The Prompting Company, Gauge. Risk: niche.
