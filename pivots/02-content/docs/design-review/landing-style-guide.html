Navy panel + dots. The two moments that ask for the click: hero and final CTA. Rounded, inset from the page edge.

Night band. Full width. Dots only where the text is short (the stance). Never behind a table.

White + grid. Only where content sits in cells (How it works).

Plain white. Everything people read closely: problem, close-ups, FAQ.

Rule of thumb: about a third of sections carry a pattern. Never two patterned sections in a row, and never a pattern behind long text.
