# chat with Daphene (Mercif, social media agency): summary

- **Recorded:** date unknown (file added 21 Oct 2026), in person, about 24 minutes
- **Transcript:** [2026-10-21-daphene-transcript.md](2026-10-21-daphene-transcript.md)
- **Context:** marketing manager (part-time, student) at [Mercif](https://www.mercif.com/), a 5-year-old Sydney social media agency for food and lifestyle brands, e.g. Evergreen Foods (tofu, sold at Woolworths) and a World Square nightclub's weekly events. The team is the founder, Daphene, one other person, a food creator, and full-time editors in Indonesia. More than 10 clients.
- **Caveats:** no speaker labels. The agency (Mercif) and tool (Cila) spellings were confirmed 25 Oct 2026; other names may be misspelled. After 21:30 the talk is personal background.

## In one line

Their content is manual and it performs (one video hit 3.5M views). They don't want AI scripts ("too robotic", and AI-scripted videos performed worse). Their biggest pain isn't making content but **tracking it across 10+ clients**, plus the research step they "don't like".

## What they do now (the full pipeline)

1. **Onboard:** branding, direction, goals (views or followers) and KPIs per client. (08:26–08:56)
2. **"1-to-10 research":** search a keyword (e.g. tofu) on Instagram and find videos with 1M+ views from small accounts (e.g. 1k followers). Those are the formats to copy. (04:33–05:11, 08:56–09:28)
3. **Qualify:** shortlist the videos to recreate. The founder approves. (09:44–10:03)
4. **Film:** Daphene plans, finds people (often passers-by) and shoots. Some content comes from paid or product-swap influencers (e.g. a ~100k-follower creator, paid). (03:24–04:08, 10:03–10:13, 14:19–14:30)
5. **Edit:** footage and instructions plus a reference video go to editors in Indonesia via a task tracker. (10:13–10:28)
6. **Review:** Daphene, then the founder. (10:28–10:47)
7. **Post and schedule:** Instagram first, reposted to TikTok, Facebook and YouTube. (09:28–09:38, 10:47–10:52)

## Pain points

- **Research.** "Research. I don't like research." (08:14–08:20) A trend-finder tool, [Cila](https://cila.app/), exists but needs a subscription, and they're limiting spend. (05:11–06:08, 10:52–11:23)
- **AI scripts underperform.** "AI ... is still, I would say, too robotic, especially in script writing ... the video doesn't really perform well if the scripting is from AI." (12:29–13:15)
- **Losing track across clients (their top wish).** Asked for one tool, they said a **project management tool**: "we have more than ten clients ... how many videos do we need, KPI, and where are we at the moment, because sometimes I lose track." Projects are "very scattered". They've hired a consultant to build a system, but it isn't finalised. (11:36–12:01, 17:37–18:56)
- **Accountability of outsourced editors.** A past editor missed deadlines and one delay holds up the whole pipeline. (20:44–21:26)
- **Not a pain:** idea or editing block. Their stage-by-stage delegation handles it. (19:46–20:38)

## Spending

They pay influencers and full-time editors, and are "investing in a tool" to simplify operations as they scale. But they're limiting spend on research subscriptions. (03:52–04:08, 11:23–11:43)

## Implications for BuildClub

- **Agencies have the clearest paid workflow, but their pain is operations, not creation.** Project tracking is a crowded market (Asana, Monday, ClickUp, etc.), so it isn't our wedge.
- **Their "1-to-10" research method is a clear, repeatable rule**: high views with low followers = a format worth copying. It could power the "trend scout" / weekly ideas step in `pivots/02-content/docs/user-flow.md` for founders who don't have a Daphene.
- **More evidence against AI-written scripts**, this time with a performance claim, not only taste.
- **The storyboard → film → edit → review → post pipeline matches our user flow almost exactly.** Agencies validate the *shape* of the flow, even if they wouldn't buy it.
